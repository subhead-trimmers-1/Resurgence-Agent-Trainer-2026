## 🎮 About the Game

The Division Resurgence is a free-to-play third-person shooter RPG and looter shooter developed and published by Ubisoft, with development led by Ubisoft Mobile Games in Paris. It launched worldwide on iOS and Android on March 31, 2026, followed by its PC release on August 5, 2026. Set in New York between the events of The Division and The Division 2, it features solo and cooperative PvE, competitive PvP, and the PvPvE Dark Zone.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **The Division Resurgence**. This trainer enhances the PC version of The Division Resurgence by providing configurable assistance for cover-based combat, weapon handling, agent survivability, skill cooldowns, movement, and PvE progression. It is designed around the game's real systems, including firearms, armor, skills, specializations, loot, open-world activities, and cooperative missions.

**Target Audience:** PC players who want a configurable training utility for solo missions, cooperative PvE activities, open-world exploration, weapon testing, and build experimentation.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the movement option to adjust traversal speed while exploring Manhattan, completing side activities, or moving between mission objectives. Keep the setting moderate during scripted sequences and cooperative play to avoid disrupting mission pacing.

### Tools & Inventory

Use inventory assistance to preserve selected ammunition and consumable resources while testing weapons or completing extended PvE activities. The game is built around collecting and upgrading weapons, gear, crafting materials, and other loot, so review your loadout before activating resource options.

### World & Environment

The open world contains story missions, side missions, daily and weekly activities, faction encounters, speed-run events, and the Dark Zone. Use trainer functions primarily in standard PvE areas, where they can support exploration and build testing without interfering with competitive modes.

### Combat & Weapons

The Division Resurgence uses third-person, cover-based gunplay combined with armor, firearms, agent skills, specializations, and tactical gadgets. Take cover, equip two useful skills, and select a specialization that matches the encounter. Combat options such as armor protection, ammunition support, no reload, and skill cooldown assistance are best used for PvE missions and controlled testing.
